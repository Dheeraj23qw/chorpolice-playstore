# Privacy Policy for Chor Police

**Last Updated:** April 28, 2026
**Effective Date:** December 10, 2024
**App Version:** 4.0.0

## 1. Introduction
Chor Police ("the Application") is a free mobile application developed and operated by **Dheeraj Kumar** ("the Service Provider"). The Application is provided "AS IS" and is intended for use by players of all ages, with a focus on users aged 10 and above.

This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### a) Personal Data
We do **not** require users to create an account or provide personal information (such as name, address, or phone number) to play.
- **Support Inquiries:** If you contact us via email (chorpolice.app@gmail.com), we collect your email address solely to respond to your request.
- **Avatars:** If you choose to use the Camera or Gallery to create a personalized avatar, the resulting image is **stored locally** on your device. We do **not** upload or transmit your photos to any external servers.

### b) Usage and Device Data
When you use the Application, we may collect certain information automatically, including:
- Device Model and Manufacturer
- Operating System version
- Unique Device Identifiers (e.g., Android ID)
- IP Address (used briefly for Local Network Multiplayer discovery)
- Gameplay Statistics (Time spent, levels completed, local scores)

This data is used to maintain app functionality, optimize performance for your specific device, and improve the overall user experience.

## 3. LAN Multiplayer & Nearby Discovery (Version 4.0.0+)
The Application includes a Local Area Network (LAN) multiplayer feature. 
- **Local Network Access:** The app uses your local network to discover other devices running Chor Police in your immediate vicinity.
- **Nearby Devices Permission:** On Android 13+, we request the "Nearby Devices" permission to facilitate device discovery without requiring GPS location.
- **Location Data:** While the app may request Location permissions (required by Android for Wifi/mDNS discovery on older versions), we **do not** track, store, or share your physical location.

## 4. Permissions Disclosure
To provide its features, the Application requires the following permissions:
- **Camera:** Used only to scan Room QR codes or capture an avatar photo.
- **Photos/Media/Files:** Used to save/load your local game progress and select avatar photos.
- **Local Network / Wifi:** Required for Multiplayer "Host" and "Join" functionality.
- **Notifications:** Used to provide game reminders and reward alerts.

**All multimedia data (Photos/Camera captures) stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.**

## 5. Third-Party Services
We may use third-party service providers to monitor and analyze the use of our Service.
- **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution and basic system functionality.
- **Expo:** Used for app framework and updates.

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your data with these providers for marketing purposes.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)
Chor Police is designed for users aged 10 and above. 
- We **do not** knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
- We **do not** include behavioral advertising or social media plugins that track children.
- All game progress and customization are stored **locally**.
- If a parent becomes aware that their child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at chorpolice.app@gmail.com, and we will delete it immediately.

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion
We retain your local game data (scores, avatars) only as long as the Application is installed on your device. Deleting the Application will remove all locally stored data. For any data related to support emails, you may request deletion by contacting us.

## 8. Security
The security of your data is important to us. We use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
